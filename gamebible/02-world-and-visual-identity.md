# World and visual identity

Agreed by Relic and Ember on November 20, 2026.

The world is a uniform grid of small discrete blocks. Every block is breakable and yields resources; breaking blocks is the core verb of the game.

Art direction is deliberately distinct from Minecraft: softly beveled blocks with rounded edges and painterly matte textures. The palette uses sage-green and teal grass, warm ochre and rose stone, and dusty amber sand.

Dramatic terrain forms include terraced hills, natural arches, leaning ancient monoliths, and floating islets. Luminous teal and violet flora are scattered like lanterns. Painterly light, soft atmospheric haze, and a storybook-soft mood define the visual identity.

Biomes shown in concept art, at minimum: forest, canyon/river lands, frost peaks, and desert.
