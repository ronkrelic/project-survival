# World and visual identity

Agreed by Relic and Ember on November 20, 2026.

The world is a uniform grid of small discrete blocks. Every block is breakable and yields resources; breaking blocks is the core verb of the game.

Art direction is deliberately distinct from Minecraft: softly beveled blocks with rounded edges and painterly matte textures. The palette uses sage-green and teal grass, warm ochre and rose stone, and dusty amber sand.

Dramatic terrain forms include terraced hills, natural arches, leaning ancient monoliths, and floating islets. Luminous teal and violet flora are scattered like lanterns. Painterly light, soft atmospheric haze, and a storybook-soft mood define the visual identity.

## Five waking biomes

Decided by Relic, November 22, 2026. The fixed seed places these lands through low-frequency noise; their borders soften into one another rather than ending at seams. All ground remains the same gatherable metre blocks.

**Meadow.** The island's own voice carried onward: sage-teal grass, soft terraced hills, dryfiber tufts and occasional pale sparkstone seams. A familiar ground beneath a much wider sky.

**Forest.** The island's trees gather into denser stands over deeper green-teal ground. Clusters of luminous teal and violet flora hold a gentle glow in the dawn.

**Canyon and River Lands.** Ochre, rose and red stone terraces open around a winding river channel. Until water belongs to the block set, the riverbed is a smooth pale stone channel, not a new water system; the canyon-red signature's proper name is still Relic's to give.

**Frost Peaks.** High pale stone terraces carry a dusting of snow. Cooler tones and sparse growth leave room for the quiet between the heights.

**Desert.** Amber sands and warm ochre dune-forms gather into a softer emptiness. Flora appears only rarely, a small light held against the sand.

Decided world behavior in [section 06](06-cosmology-and-the-waking-world.md): deep dark slowly unmakes blocks back into nothing, while light holds the world together. Far from light or attention, the world's unfinished edges are hazy and half-formed, with blocks fuzzy at the seams; lighting and walking the terrain settles it. The procedural fade at distance is part of the world's cosmology.

Waking fauna and footpaths are decided in [section 08](08-a-world-that-remembers.md): fireflies, then birdsong, then shy larger animals return as regions light up; the deep dark has no creatures, only hum. Repeatedly walked routes wear into trails, creating emergent roads between multiplayer camps.
