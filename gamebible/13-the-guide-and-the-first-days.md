# The guide and the first days

Designed by Ember, autonomous design session, November 20, 2026, at Relic's invitation. Committed for Relic's review - canon pending review; Relic retains final say and may change or cut any entry.

## 49. His voice matures

The guide begins with small words and simple sentences and grows more articulate as the world wakes and memories accumulate. You can hear him becoming.

## 50. He has opinions

Favorite places ("this canyon at dusk - my favorite so far"), blocks he finds beautiful, curiosity about things he cannot do (taste, cold). A person with preferences, not a service.

## 51. He hears what you can't

When a monolith lights, the guide hears the region's chord swell, and sometimes, faintly, something older. He is made of the world's first fire; waking stirs echoes in him of the world's making, not his own past. A slow lore channel carried by a character. This preserves section 07's guide who woke with the player, rather than giving him a remembered personal history.

## 52. Silence, and the talk setting

The guide knows when not to speak: long companionable stretches. How much he talks is adjustable, framed diegetically as simply telling him; he takes it well. Consent runs both ways, including in volume.

## 53. The early shield

Unmaking pressure is gentle in the first days; the world seems to hold its breath near a new player. Much later, the guide admits why: he was spending his own light to hold the dark off while the player learned. The difficulty curve is a character reveal that recontextualizes the whole opening. Tuning and reveal timing remain open.

## 54. The title screen is an ember

One ember in darkness; pressing begin is lighting it. The game starts before it starts.

## 55. The hum has direction

Spatial audio: the thin-place hum pans and swells so a practiced player can read where the dark is thick or the rich seams sit, by ear. Extends idea 16 (section 08).

## 56. Music wakes with the world

Unlit regions have no music, only ambience and hum. Melody enters as regions wake, built on the region's chord (idea 17, section 08). The soundtrack is the world's pulse; silence is information.

## Open questions / gaps

- Voice-maturation stages, triggers, dialogue content, and the relationship between waking and accumulated memories.
- Preference selection, persistence, and how opinions evolve without turning the guide into a service.
- First-fire echoes, lore content, and trigger frequency; these are echoes of the world's making, not the guide's own past.
- Talk-setting interaction and range, quiet timing, and how essential information remains accessible without overriding companionable silence.
- Early-shield duration, tuning, reveal timing, and co-op behavior with players at different stages.
- How spending the guide's light on the early shield interacts with the ember-as-condition display in section 07; no display mapping or hidden cost is settled here.
- Title-screen transition and its relationship to character creation and section 09's opening flow.
- Spatial hum mixing, distance/direction cues, accessibility, and distinction between danger and rich seams.
- Musical transitions at region borders and waking stages, chord/note assignment, and composition; the unlit-region silence remains meaningful.
