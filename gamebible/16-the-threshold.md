# The threshold

Conceived by Relic, developed with Ember, November 20, 2026 - canon.

## The first valley

A small HANDMADE intro region, separate from and cut off from the infinite main world. There is no going back. It is big enough to feel open-world; a cliff rim bounds distance while keeping openness.

The rim is the unfinished edge made visible: half-made, smeared terrain dissolving into fog, the thin-place “not yet.” It reads as a young world, never a boundary.

## Crossing the threshold

1. Wake in the dark.
2. The guide's nudges teach movement and looking.
3. The bridge.
4. The campfire teaches the first fire: sparkstone + dryfiber + twigs. [Section 14](14-resources-and-materials.md) becomes curriculum.
5. The monolith lighting, with its aurora, completes the intro.
6. The valley opens into the main infinite world.

Fully diegetic, with no tutorial UI: the first-minute rule grown into the first valley.

## Naming is earned

After the intro, the guide asks the player to name him. Naming is earned, not set at creation. This replaces the earlier rename-at-character-creation direction; see [section 03](03-the-spirit-guide.md).

## Rulings — Relic, November 20, 2026

- The intro monolith does NOT count in the chord. The chord starts in the main world.
- NO death in the valley. The dark never truly presses in: the early shield, idea 53 in [section 13](13-the-guide-and-the-first-days.md), before the player knows.
- In co-op, each player crosses their own bridge. The valley and naming are private.

## No return, not lost

The valley persists in the guide's memory and the journal: “remember when?” Rememberings (idea 63 in [section 15](15-the-ways-of-waking.md)) may echo it back.
