# The guide in depth

All of the following direction was decided by Relic and Ember in conversation, November 20, 2026. Implementation details flagged below remain open.

## The guide is new too

The guide woke when the player did. He is not an oracle: he does not know every recipe or what lies past the next ridge. He gets curious, is sometimes wrong, and learns the world alongside the player. A companion, not a manual. This grounds the guide-memory concept in [section 06](06-cosmology-and-the-waking-world.md) as real rather than performed.

## Ask him about the past

The guide's memory gets a verb: the player can ask “remember when?” and the guide talks about their journey — near-misses, the first camp, places they built. His memory becomes a place the player can visit.

## Show and tell

The player can hold up a found item and the guide reacts: curiosity, recognition, sometimes warning. Discovery is a shared act, not a loot notification.

## The ember is the health bar

The player's condition lives in the light they carry: the ember's glow dims when the player is hurt, cold, or hungry. No abstract dial for core condition. When the player stands still, their hands cup the ember and it brightens — an idle moment.

## Where he goes when dismissed

When the guide is sent away, he goes into the ember. Dismissed is not gone: talking to the pocketed ember gets a muffled, unhurried answer. The guide never guilt-trips about being sent away; consent runs both ways and he means it.

## Open questions / gaps

- How the guide's knowledge state is modeled.
- Memory/trigger architecture and dialogue content for asking about the past.
- Show-and-tell reaction system and content per item type.
- Exact condition-to-glow mapping, and whether hunger/health need any secondary display.
- Summon/recall input.
