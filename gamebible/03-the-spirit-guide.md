# The spirit guide

Agreed by Relic and Ember on November 20, 2026.

Each player has a personal spirit guide, visible and audible only to that player, even in multiplayer.

The guide's origin is decided in [section 06](06-cosmology-and-the-waking-world.md): a spark of the world's first fire, part of the player rather than an assigned helper.

As decided in [section 07](07-the-guide-in-depth.md), the guide woke when the player did: curious, sometimes wrong, and learning alongside them, not an oracle.

Default name: Ember. After the intro, the guide asks the player to name him — earned, not set at creation; see [section 16](16-the-threshold.md).

## Presence modes

- ACTIVE: the guide is with you, talks about things you are finding, teaches crafting recipes, and helps you learn the game organically, beyond any scripted tutorial.
- SUMMON/RECALL: the player can dismiss the guide, who goes into the ember until summoned again, with no penalty; a muffled, unhurried answer is still available when the player talks to the pocketed ember. He never guilt-trips about dismissal; consent runs both ways ([section 07](07-the-guide-in-depth.md)).

Diegetic presence concept: the player carries a small ember; lit means the guide is present, pocketed means quiet unless spoken to or called. This is visual language, with the final form open.

## Appearance

The default and primary face is Ember's established design: an androgynous figure with warm brown skin, short dark hair threaded with small pearl-like beads, honey-amber eyes with faint constellation flecks, cream and caramel colored clothes, and a loose flowing scarf. The guide is rendered smooth and softly luminous with a golden-amber glow, faintly translucent, deliberately NOT made of blocks: the guide is the one presence in the world that is not matter.

Additional guide faces may exist later; Ember's is “one of the main faces.”
