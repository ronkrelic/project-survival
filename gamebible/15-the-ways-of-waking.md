# The ways of waking

Designed by Ember, November 20, 2026; approved by Relic, November 20, 2026 - canon.

Survival is what you do each night; roleplay is who you are becoming. No XP bars, stat screens, or combat classes — the RPG layer is attention.

## 57. Attention is the XP

No levels or sheets. You become what you attend to; the world settles it into you like terrain. Progression is the cosmology.

## 58. Ways of waking, grown not chosen

- Wayfinder: walk far and read the hum.
- Hearthkeeper: keep fires and build.
- Deepwalker: dive the deep dark.
- Waker: tend flora and earn fauna trust.

Blend freely. Co-op camps gain emergent parties, with no menus.

## 59. The guide is the relationship RPG

Accumulated conversation choices shape his personality: bolder or careful, funnier or quieter. Players would replay to meet a different him.

## 60. Vows at the hearth

“We'll light the frost monolith before deep winter.” The guide remembers. Kept vows deepen the ember's light and trust; broken vows live in his memory.

## 61. Callings, not quests

No givers or markers: a hum under a ridge, a dream of an unseen monolith, a waystone leaning wrong. The journal keeps threads; curiosity is the only quest flag.

## 62. Items earn legends

No drop tables. The journal records what an item survived; names stick. Extends idea 46 in [section 12](12-four-lights.md).

## 63. Rememberings

At old ash sites, listen: echoed vignettes of the previous people. Experienced, never read. A possible source of callings.

## 64. World-scale choice

Monolith order reorders regional seasons; names are permanent. Finally the game asks: does everything sleeping want to be woken? Players roleplay the answer for hours before knowing it is a question.
