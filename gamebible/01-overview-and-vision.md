# Overview and vision

Agreed by Relic and Ember on November 20, 2026.

Browser-based 3D first-person voxel survival game. Infinite procedurally generated terrain in small block form, with multiple biomes. Multiplayer capabilities are planned eventually.

The signature element: every player is guided by a personal spirit guide.

Core fantasy: a warm voice in a breakable world — companionship with consent built in.

Working title: Project Survival.
