# Living with the dark

Designed by Ember, autonomous design session, November 20, 2026, at Relic's invitation. Fully approved by Relic, November 20, 2026 - official canon.

## 37. The ember grows

The player's personal light radius starts small and grows as they climb the fire ladder (hearth-fed, beacon-fed; section 08). Late game, you carry a lantern of a soul. Early tension comes from a small radius, not resource drains.

## 38. Carrying light

Portable fire up the ladder (spark, torch, lantern) burns down while carrying your radius. Deep dives become a logistics of light: bring enough, or race the hum.

## 39. The recipe book is a field journal

Discovered recipes render as sketched entries in the storybook hand, annotated in the guide's own phrasing. The UI is an object that belongs to the world. Discovery remains player-led, as in section 06.

## 40. Evenings by the fire

Tools wear visibly and are repaired at the fire at night: a cozy ritual loop that gives nights purpose beyond fear. Sleep, repair, talk, plan.

## 41. Cold is the dark's touch

Deep dark is also deep cold; warmth comes from fire and shelter. Cold feeds the condition system the ember already displays (section 07); the exact relationship is open.

## 42. Materials argue with the dark

Some blocks resist unmaking but smother light (deep stone); some carry light further than it should go (a glow-wood or lantern-vein). Architecture becomes strategy: line a hall with light-carriers and one hearth lights it all.

## 43. No claim flags

Land is never locked. A lit hearth simply holds its radius; in co-op, what stands inside someone's light is theirs by convention, not code. Systems trust players. This is canon alongside the claim-stake language in sections 04 and 06, not an edit to those sections.

## Open questions / gaps

- Personal-radius growth thresholds, tier relationships, and interaction with condition dimming in section 07.
- Portable-fire burn times and replenishment; how the logistics of idea 38 coexist with idea 37's early tension without resource drains and section 09's away fires that never fully die.
- Field-journal layout, annotations, and recipe content.
- Tool wear, repair costs and inputs, and the fire tiers required for repairs.
- Cold accumulation, shelter qualification, warmth recovery, and the exact relationship to the ember condition display.
- Material names, resistance and light-carrying values, propagation limits, and resource distribution; glow-wood and lantern-vein are possibilities, not a settled material table.
- Co-op social conventions and implementation implications of land remaining unlocked; no coded ownership system is specified here.
