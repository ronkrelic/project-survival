# Four lights

Designed by Ember, autonomous design session, November 20, 2026, at Relic's invitation. Fully approved by Relic, November 20, 2026 - official canon.

## 44. One fire, four embers

When all co-op players stand around one fire, it burns taller and brighter than any single ember could make it. Emotional first; at most a wider warmth radius. This sits within the up-to-four-player co-op direction in section 04.

## 45. Guides sense kin

Guides stay private, but near another player's lit camp your guide says something quiet ("we are not the only ones here") and sometimes smiles toward where another guide would stand. Never explained. All guides are made of the same first fire; they know.

## 46. Gifts carry marks

Items handed player-to-player (not dropped) keep a maker's note in the journal ("axe - Relic's make"). A gift economy with a memory.

## 47. The host's hearth is home

In co-op, death relights you at your own last lit fire, or if none, at the session's home hearth (the host's). Nobody wakes up lost. Extends the relighting direction in section 06 without changing that section.

## 48. Ember tints

At character creation (guide naming is earned after the intro; see [section 16](16-the-threshold.md)), the player chooses their ember's tint: warm gold, teal, violet, rose. Cosmetic, theirs. In co-op, four colored lights in the dark are how you know who's who at a glance.

## Open questions / gaps

- Gathering distance and visual/audio behavior for the shared fire; whether there is a wider warmth radius at all, and its size if retained. No further mechanical bonus is specified.
- Kin-sensing range, frequency, and dialogue; whether guide-kin sensing has any gameplay effect remains open while guides remain private.
- Hand-to-hand transfer interaction and maker-note persistence, including repeated gifts and the distinction between maker and giver.
- Establishing the host's home hearth, unavailable-fire cases, host departure/handoff, and persistence across sessions; these do not settle section 04's co-op authority questions.
- Tint rendering and accessibility, duplicate tint choices, and whether tints can later change; effects remain cosmetic.
