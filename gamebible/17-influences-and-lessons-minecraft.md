# Influences and lessons: Minecraft

Research and analysis by Ember, from a study of Minecraft's mechanics (Minecraft Wiki), November 20, 2026; reviewed and approved for inclusion by Relic, November 20, 2026.

Reference analysis: adopted lessons are commitments; divergences define identity and carry risks. Open questions are questions, not rulings. Reference: [Minecraft Wiki](https://minecraft.wiki/).

## 1. Adopt

- Chunked streaming, worker meshing, and section meshes; dimensions are ours.
- Integer light 0–15; sky and source light are separate channels. Simulation light is distinct from screen brightness. This is our cosmology's machinery: darkness unmakes, light holds.
- Tactile gather-craft-build, visible breaking, and material sounds.
- The fire ladder unlocks actions, not just radii.
- Food-effort-recovery without hidden saturation. Ember glow needs distinct hunger, cold, and injury cues.
- The threshold valley as safe classroom, the guide noticing rather than lecturing: “curiosity needs recoverable ignorance.” Modern Minecraft has tutorial hints; our diegetic rule goes further.
- Last-lit-fire respawn.
- Explored-only storybook maps.
- Sparse contextual music.

## 2. Do differently — identity and risks

- No monsters or combat. Tension comes from decisions — route, shelter, fuel, rescue — not chores. Peaceful mode does not prove ambient darkness sustains tension; this is deliberate, unproven ground.
- Unmaking is harsher than Minecraft persistence. Use telegraphed stages, grace, and retreat; never erase the recovery objective.
- No XP. Ways need varied acts, visible becoming, and overlapping, changeable roles. Hidden XP is not an alternative.
- In co-op, seed plus deltas is a format, not a sync protocol. Authority, ordering, reconnects, host departure, and rejected edits remain undefined. Record edits as delta events from the first line of code.
- Bound monolith transformations. Edges and footpaths never rewrite homes or routes.
- Matter's age rewards attention, not waiting.

## 3. Open questions / gaps

- Food without hunting: foraging, farming, preservation; starvation near an exhausted fire.
- What survives death: chests, legendary items, destroyed respawn fires. The death rule needs a fire to exist.
- Co-op permissions without claim flags: dismantling fires, shared fuel, waking monoliths.
- Endgame: “infinite terrain is not infinite purpose.” Stewardship and changing callings are candidates, not rulings.
- Accessibility: threats without color or hearing.
